## Full Description
* **Limit**: Max 4000 characters
* **Current Length**: ~1550 characters

```text
Ever wondered how many meters your thumb travels every day? Discover your screen habits with ScrollTrek, the ultimate scrolling distance tracker designed to turn your screen time into a premium milestone journey.

ScrollTrek dynamically tracks your physical scrolling activity in the background, transforming mindless scrolling into an active trek. Reach real-world landmarks, build healthy digital habits, and challenge yourself to rest your eyes!

KEY FEATURES:

📊 REAL-TIME DISTANCE TRACKING
Calculate your cumulative daily scrolling distance in meters. Observe your stats update in real-time as you browse your favorite apps.

🏆 UNLOCK WORLD LANDMARKS
Embark on a virtual journey. Scroll the equivalent height of the Eiffel Tower, the depth of the Grand Canyon, or the height of Mount Everest. Every milestone reached sends a premium achievement notification.

🔥 DAILY STREAKS & GOALS
Set a personalized daily scroll distance target. Maintain streaks by hitting your goal every day, and build healthy limits.

📉 WEEKLY INSIGHTS & ANALYTICS
Visualize your trends with dynamic, beautifully styled bar charts. Easily identify which days had the heaviest usage.

🔒 AUTO-EXCLUSION FOR DATA PRIVACY
We take your privacy seriously. ScrollTrek automatically disables tracking on sensitive applications such as banking, finance, and health apps. Additionally, apps with secure windows (like secure chat portals) are shielded by Android.

🛡️ 100% SECURE & LOCAL STORAGE
Zero data leaves your device. All distance computations are processed locally and stored in a secure on-device database. No tracking scripts, no cloud storage, and absolutely no external data sharing.

HOW IT WORKS:
ScrollTrek uses a lightweight Android Accessibility Service to safely calculate scrolling movement based on coordinate updates. It runs efficiently in the background via a persistent notification without draining your battery.

Take back control of your screen time and start your journey today with ScrollTrek!
```
